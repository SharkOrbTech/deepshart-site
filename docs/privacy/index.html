Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy describes how the DeepShart app and website handle information. Contact SharkOrbTech at hi@sharkorb.com for privacy requests.

Data on your device

DeepShart stores conversations, settings, memories, selected attachments and local backups on your device. It uses them to display, search, import, export and restore your data. On iPhone, your DeepSeek API key is stored in the system Keychain.

The app has no DeepShart account registration, advertising tracking or built-in analytics.

DeepSeek API

When you use DeepSeek, the app uses your API key to authenticate and sends your prompts, relevant chat history, memories and selected attachments as needed for replies, web search, summaries and memory extraction. Testing the connection, loading available models and checking quota also send authenticated requests.

DeepSeek processes requests and connection information under the applicable DeepSeek Open Platform Terms of Service and account arrangements. Information may be processed in mainland China or other jurisdictions used by DeepSeek. Retention and further processing depend on the arrangements that apply to the account. Review any personal information in your input before sending it.

Tencent Cloud COS

On Android and desktop, you can configure COS to upload and download conversation snapshots, backups and sync deletion records. The app uses your credentials to sign requests to the selected bucket. Automatic sync runs according to the schedule you set. You control the bucket region, access permissions and retention settings.

Device sync

DeepShart uses WebRTC to exchange conversation data, snapshots, backups and sync records with devices you have paired. The other device can see its peer’s device name and identifier during pairing.

PeerJS Cloud provides signaling. Tencent, Xiaomi, Bilibili and Google provide STUN services used to establish connections. These services may receive network addresses and the signaling information needed to connect.

Permissions, retention and deletion

On iPhone, authorize DeepSeek and device sync separately under Global Settings → About → Privacy & Data before using each service. You can withdraw these permissions at any time. Withdrawal stops the app from starting new transfers to that service. Previously sent copies follow the storage and retention settings of the recipient.

You can delete conversations and attachments, remove local backups, and clear a saved API key by emptying the field and saving. On Android and desktop, you can clear COS credentials in the COS settings. To remove copies from a paired device or COS bucket, manage them at that destination. Contact DeepSeek about information it holds.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts the website and records visitors’ IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website keeps language and theme choices in your browser and uses no advertising or analytics scripts.

If you contact us, we use the address and details you provide to respond and retain them as needed to resolve your request or meet legal obligations. You can contact hi@sharkorb.com about access, corrections, deletion or other privacy rights.
