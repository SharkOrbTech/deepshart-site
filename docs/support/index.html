Support

Contact SharkOrbTech for app support, privacy requests or reports about generated content.

Email

Write to hi@sharkorb.com. Include your platform and app version, then describe what happened. Keep API keys, cloud credentials and private conversation data out of your message.

Report an AI reply

Describe the issue and share only the excerpt needed to explain it. We use what you share to review and respond to the report.

Privacy requests

Describe the information and the action you are requesting. The Privacy policy explains how to remove local data and copies held by connected services.
